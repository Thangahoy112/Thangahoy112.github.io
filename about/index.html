I’m Thang, and I live in the Chicago area. I studied business and marketing at Benedictine University, then worked my way through retail management, a financial services call center, a mountain retreat center, a local dental practice’s website and insurance recruiting. Each one taught me something about people, and about the simple systems that help a team do its best work. I’m still learning every day, lately with AI tools and creative projects.
