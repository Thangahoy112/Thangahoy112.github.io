01

The story

Quinnie Dental Care is a small dental practice in Modesto, California. From 2022 to 2025 I worked with them remotely as a project, alongside my full-time job.

A small practice doesn’t have a marketing department, so I tried to be one. I built their website from scratch in Wix, helped people find it on Google, ran their social media, and designed the flyers and seasonal emails that told patients about new offers.

Everything in the work samples below is my own work: two pages of the live website and two of my flyers. No patient information appears here.

02

What I did

  • Designed and built the whole Wix website myself, including the layout, the Home, About Us, Gallery and Special Discounts pages, and a clear Book Now button on the first screen. Over the same years, the practice grew its customers by 38% a year on average.
  • Wrote and improved the website copy and on-page SEO, and organic traffic grew 35% year over year. I sent a Google Analytics report every month so we could see what was working.
  • Designed the promotional flyers, like the teeth-whitening and new-patient offers below, and the seasonal email campaigns.
  • Created and posted the practice’s social media content.
  • Kept the website running smoothly and fixed technical issues when they came up.

06

Reflection

A small practice doesn’t have a marketing team, so I learned to be one, and to spend my hours on what actually brings people through the door. That meant a website that answers questions quickly, offers that are easy to understand, and showing up steadily where people already look. The monthly Google Analytics reports kept me honest about what was working, and taught me that steady care often matters more than a big budget.
