01

The story

FIS (Fidelity National Information Services) is a financial technology company. From August 2019 to February 2022 I supervised a remote customer service team that helped people with their 401(k) retirement accounts.

When someone calls about their retirement savings, it matters to them. My job was to help my team give clear, correct answers and feel supported while doing it. No customer or company-confidential information appears here.

02

What I did

  • Supervised and coached 25+ representatives. Productivity rose 30%, and we consistently hit 98% of our department’s KPI targets.
  • Looked after 10,000+ customer conversations a month, with 95% customer satisfaction and 87% of questions solved on the first call.
  • Brought in quality checks, training and KPI reporting. Escalated complaints dropped 35% and average handling time fell 20%, while we stayed within financial services rules.
