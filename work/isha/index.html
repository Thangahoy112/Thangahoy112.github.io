The story

From 2016 to 2025 I volunteered remotely with Isha Foundation North America, helping with social media, content and community management. It was volunteer work I did alongside my jobs, not a staff role.

With 480+ volunteers involved, good coordination made all the difference. Much of my part was quiet, behind-the-scenes work: keeping calendars, approvals and handoffs clear so content for Sadhguru’s channels could go out on time.

Save Soil (2022). Sadhguru set out on a 100-day motorcycle journey from the United Kingdom to India, through 27 countries, to rally support for healthier soil. I helped the message travel further by reaching out to influencers and creators, making and posting social media content, and helping organize events and the volunteers who ran them.

Cauvery Calling. This campaign helps farmers in southern India move to tree-based farming to bring a river back to life. I supported its awareness work alongside the rest of the team.

The campaign links below go to the organization’s own sites. They show the causes I worked for, not pieces I made myself.

02

What I did

  • Coordinated content calendars, creative assets, approvals and publishing across six brands and channels, which together reach 14M+ followers and subscribers.
  • Helped with Sadhguru’s social media, YouTube and blog content, kept tasks and approvals moving in ClickUp, and wrote workflow guides for 480+ volunteers.
  • For Save Soil in 2022, reached out to influencers and creators to share the campaign, made and posted social media content, and helped organize events and coordinate volunteers.
  • Pitched in on media outreach, landing pages, email and awareness campaigns, including Cauvery Calling.

05

Reflection

Volunteers don’t show up for a paycheck. They show up because they believe in the cause. That taught me my job wasn’t to push people. It was to clear the path, with a clear calendar, a quick approval and a guide anyone could pick up and follow. When the work felt easy to join, people gave their very best, and I carry that into every team I work with.
